# Website imagery

The site uses two scenes generated with the built-in ImageGen tool. They do not show completed RRV projects, specific employees, or verified worksites. Both scenes are labeled as illustrations on the site.

- [Desktop hero](../public/assets/construction-highlands.webp) and [mobile crop](../public/assets/construction-highlands-small.webp): “Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: wide editorial photography hero for a Madagascar construction contractor website. Primary request: high-end architectural documentary photograph of a real-looking active but temporarily quiet reinforced-concrete structure under construction in the Malagasy highlands outside Antananarivo. Raw gray concrete slab, columns and exposed rebar; some porous terracotta brick infill, orderly stacks and dry rust-red earth. The structure is a simple modern two-storey build, not ruins. Soft small terracotta roofs and green folds of hills on the horizon, left side recedes into shadow. Main structure on right half and middle, some clean atmospheric sky; left third reasonably calm and dark for an HTML typographic overlay. Low eye-level wide-angle scene, full bleed landscape 16:9, restrained warm ochre and olive tones, hazy late-afternoon sun from upper right. Rich but unsentimental, tactile and architectural; no tower cranes, no words, no logos, no collage, no watermark. Make the structure and hillside visibly readable, with a calm cinematic composition.”
- [Site planning](../public/assets/site-planning.webp): “Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: editorial site-process photo for a Madagascar construction contractor website. A candid documentary medium shot on a highland construction site near Antananarivo, Madagascar: two Malagasy construction professionals in dusty cream safety helmets quietly study a simple unbranded building plan laid flat together on a rough waist-high timber work table. Natural bodies and hands, loose long-sleeve olive and rust cotton work clothes. Worker on left bends slightly toward the sheet; worker on right seen in three-quarter profile. Gray masonry, vertical raw concrete formwork, distant eucalyptus and soft daylight in the background. Landscape 4:3 composition with subjects near center and breathing room on all sides, eye height, tactile film texture, subtle neutral color grade with slightly warm grays, realistic outdoor light. No readable text, no logos, no banners, no watermark; looks like real architectural editorial photography.”

The generated originals were resized and encoded as WebP for the website. When replacing these with RRV photographs, update their captions and alt text to match.
